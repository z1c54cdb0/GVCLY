About

Spindle Thread Loft is a casual game hub themed around a textile loft — rhythm faders, runway jumps, crate puzzles, and silo shifts.

Theme story: spindle_thread_loft. All gameplay runs offline in your browser with local saves only.

Privacy

No account is required. Progress and preferences are stored with localStorage on this device under keys prefixed with your app scope id.

We do not collect gameplay analytics in this web bundle. Clearing site data removes your saves.

Contact: support@example.com (replace with your App Store support URL before release).
